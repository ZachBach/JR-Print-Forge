Tolerance FAQ
What tolerance can you actually hold?

Typically ±0.10 mm on FDM features and tighter on resin. Anything critical gets called out on the quote before we print, not after.

Do press-fits and threads come out to size?

Tell us the mating part. We compensate holes and bosses per material shrink, or design in heat-set inserts where threads take load.

What if the first article is wrong?

Send it back with the measurement. If it missed the tolerance we quoted, the reprint is on us.
